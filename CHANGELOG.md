## 1.0.0

Initial reproducibility archive for the paper 22 validation of the allmeta E-value page against EValue 4.1.4 (22,428/22,428 checks on 3,738 configurations, 26/26 refusals, no app defect). The allmeta release identifiers are filled: v1.8.0 (tag commit 1cb3f84, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23515177). The archive version in CITATION.cff and .zenodo.json is 1.0.0; it previously carried the allmeta release token. RELEASE_EQUIVALENCE.md records that `app-v1.8.0/` is byte-identical to the v1.8.0 blobs, and that replaying the corpus on those bytes reproduces the archived rows exactly. The verifier now requires the filled values and the equivalence record. Machine paths in five archive-authored files are replaced by `<WORKSPACE>`, and an internal status file in `publication/` is removed. README.md, CITATION.cff and .zenodo.json now use LF line endings. SOURCE_SHA256.json is re-pinned.

Release tree and metadata, before the first public release: the archive's creators are Mahmood Ahmad only, as for the other allmeta validation archives. The co-authors, their unconfirmed affiliations and an internal note were removed from `.zenodo.json` and `CITATION.cff`; the paper carries the full author list. Internal workflow files were removed from the release tree: the whole `publication/` directory (manuscript working files and checker, the numerical trace, a release-status note and target-journal notes), `references/author_distribution_2-22.json` (author allocation), `references/NOVELTY_WORDING_2-19.md` (internal wording rules) and `references/verification/journal.md` with `references/verification/journal/` (saved target-journal pages). The verifier did not pin these files by name; SOURCE_SHA256.json is re-pinned without them.

History: the published history keeps every development commit, its order and its timestamps, rewritten with redactions (`git filter-repo`). The machine paths, the internal files listed above and the co-author entries and internal note in the historical metadata are removed from every commit. Commit ids changed: the preregistration commits are now 444eb16 (was ea69964) and 4f8ed9a (was 2f5279f), and the replayed commits are 81f99f2 (was 682b73a) and 526f14c (was 6a7d71b). The replay evidence directories and files are renamed to the new ids; their `.meta` files keep the ids of the runs. The verifier now proves the preregistration order from the git history and requires the new `PREREG_RECORD.json` to agree; a squashed or shallow history fails. `test_safeguards.py` adds five controls for this check (9/9). SOURCE_SHA256.json is re-pinned.
